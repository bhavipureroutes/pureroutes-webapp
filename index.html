Connect institutional food buyers with hyperlocal sourcing networks and smart logistics — making Tier 2 & Tier 3 food supply as predictable as metro operations.

Our Services

A comprehensive B2B platform tailored for restaurant chains and cloud kitchens in non-metro areas

Structured Procurement Governance

We design and manage controlled procurement frameworks using existing local and regional suppliers — with defined quality standards, fallback logic, and escalation rules.
Result: consistent supply without daily vendor firefighting.

Partner Led Logistics Orchestration

Rather than owning trucks, we govern delivery behaviour through route discipline, cut-off controls, and performance SLAs across partner logistics networks.
Result: reliable last-mile execution without asset lock-in.

Tech Enabled Control Systems

Centralised ordering, visibility, and exception handling — built for teams managing multiple cities simultaneously.
Result: fewer surprises as footprint expands.

City-Wise Planning Intelligence

Operational insights derived from non-metro execution — including category volatility, substitution planning, and city-specific constraints.
Result: better expansion decisions before issues surface.

Expansion-Ready Commercial Structuring

Result: launches move faster without compromising control.

Hyperlocal Replication Playbooks
